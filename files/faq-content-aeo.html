Frequently Asked Questions

What does Velnix Solutions do?

Velnix Solutions helps growing businesses automate internal workflows with AI, custom software, and intelligent systems that improve efficiency and scale operations.

Who is AI workflow automation for?

AI workflow automation is built for growing businesses that want to reduce repetitive manual work, cut operational costs, and free up staff time for higher-value tasks without adding headcount.

What is the difference between AI automation and custom software?

Custom software is built to fit a specific business process from the ground up. AI automation adds intelligent decision-making, such as natural language processing or computer vision, on top of existing or new systems to handle tasks that previously required manual judgment.
